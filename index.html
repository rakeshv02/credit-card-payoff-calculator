What is the Credit Card Payoff Calculator?

The Credit Card Payoff Calculator shows how long it will take to clear your balance and how much interest you will pay, based on your APR and monthly payment. It reveals the real cost of carrying a balance and how faster payments save money.

How it works

Interest accrues on the outstanding balance each month at monthly rate = APR ÷ 12. Each payment first covers that month's interest, and the remainder reduces the principal. The tool repeats this month by month until the balance reaches zero, tracking total interest along the way.

Example 1: A $5,000 balance at 20% APR with $150/month takes about 47 months to clear and costs roughly $1,950 in interest.
Example 2: The same $5,000 at 20% APR but paying $300/month clears in about 19 months with only around $900 in interest — doubling the payment more than halves the cost.

When to use it

Use it to see how long minimum payments would really take, to compare the savings from paying an extra $50 or $100 a month, to plan a debt-free date, or to decide whether a balance transfer to a lower rate is worthwhile.
